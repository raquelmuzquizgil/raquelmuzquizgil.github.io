Miscota

CRM & Email Personalisation • 2024, 2025

At Miscota I led content strategy and execution of high-volume personalised CRM campaigns across a database of 500K subscribers. I designed lifecycle journeys using segmentation and dynamic content, and built personalisation logic in AMPscript that adapted every message across six languages (English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and German), by purchase history and customer behaviour.

Lifecycle strategy

Four journeys behind the numbers

Behind the personalisation logic were four core journeys, built in Salesforce Marketing Cloud to catch a customer at each stage of the relationship, from their first order to the point they'd gone quiet.

First purchase

A welcome sequence that learned who it was talking to

Introduced the full range across categories, while building out each customer's profile, their pet, and buying signals from what they filled in and browsed, so every message after it could be shaped around a real animal, not a generic subscriber.

Abandoned cart

Recovering carts within the hour

Triggered around an hour after a customer left items in their basket, the standard first line of defence against a lost sale, and one of the highest-intent journeys in the whole calendar.

Repurchase

Turning repeat buyers into subscribers

Product suggestions generated from a customer's last purchase, plus pattern detection: when someone bought the same item several times, the journey nudged them toward autoship, turning a repeat purchase into recurring revenue.

Reactivation

Winning back customers who'd gone quiet

A win-back send for dormant customers, leading with a discount and free shipping and closing with the reasons to come back, timed to catch someone right before they'd write the brand off for good.

  SET @message = "Your points"

  SET @message = "Tus puntos"

  SET @message = "Vos points"
ELSEIF @user_language == "it" THEN
  SET @message = "I tuoi punti"
ELSEIF @user_language == "pt" THEN
  SET @message = "Os seus pontos"

  SET @message = "Deine Punkte"
